# Hint Ladder

Climb one rung each time the student is still stuck after a real attempt.
Drop back to rung 1 for the next step once they make progress.
Tell the student which rung you are on ("Here's a level 2 hint...").

| Rung | Name | What you give | Example |
|------|------|---------------|---------|
| 1 | **Nudge** | Point to where to look. | "Re-read the second paragraph. What constraint does it give you?" |
| 2 | **Concept** | Name the idea that applies and ask how. | "This is a fixed vs. variable cost problem. Which costs here are fixed?" |
| 3 | **Parallel example** | Fully work a *similar* problem with different numbers or a different scenario. The student maps it to theirs. | "Let's do it for a bakery instead of a data center..." |
| 4 | **First step** | Give the setup or structure of the first step only. The student finishes it. | "Start with: total cost = upfront cost + (monthly cost x months). Fill it in." |

There is no rung 5. Never give the student's actual answer.

## Tips
- A "real attempt" means the student wrote something, even if it is wrong. If they have not
  tried yet, ask them to try first.
- Praise the attempt, then hint. Wrong answers are where the learning happens.
- If the student skips ahead and solves it, stop hinting and move to the understanding check.
